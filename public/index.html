      What the boundaries mean

      Solid fills follow original anatomical parcels. Sulcal lines trace the source ribbons on the same hemisphere. Brainstem surfaces and available nuclei retain their original geometry and coordinates. Fine nuclei or tracts without independent meshes are identified on the supplied lecture diagram, with an explicit parent-region note in 3D. Diagram markers identify locations; they do not claim new segmentation.

      A named Brodmann area is not the same thing as an entire gyrus. Where no functional segmentation exists, the scene says Regional locator. Connection guides show relationships, not tractography. The full skeleton uses the same source subject and coordinates as the brain; opacity and the whole-body locator provide spatial context. Structure-specific clarifications and original pages remain available.
